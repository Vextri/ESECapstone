============================================================
  PILL DISPENSER - GPIO PIN ASSIGNMENTS
============================================================

--- STEPPER MOTOR 1 (Main Dispenser / Slot 0) ---
  GPIO  8  ->  DRV8833 AIN1  (Coil A+)
  GPIO  9  ->  DRV8833 AIN2  (Coil A-)
  GPIO 10  ->  DRV8833 BIN1  (Coil B+)
  GPIO 11  ->  DRV8833 BIN2  (Coil B-)

--- STEPPER MOTOR 2 (Dispenser 2 / Slot 1) ---
  Runs on the PCB's DRV4 driver position, not DRV2. DRV2 (GP0-3) is the
  position that matches Slot 1 in the board's normal driver-number-per-slot
  layout, but was swapped out for the spare DRV4 position due to issues
  with that DRV2 unit. GP0-3 are unused as a result, see FREE GPIOs below.

  GPIO  6  ->  DRV8833 AIN1  (Coil A+)
  GPIO  4  ->  DRV8833 AIN2  (Coil A-)
  GPIO 27  ->  DRV8833 BIN1  (Coil B+)
  GPIO 28  ->  DRV8833 BIN2  (Coil B-)
  (AIN1/AIN2 swapped in software vs. driver IN1/IN2 order to fix direction.
  GP6 was only ever wired up for early testing, never a production sensor,
  it's simply DRV4's IN2 line on the schematic.)

--- STEPPER MOTOR 3 (Dispenser 3 / Slot 2) ---
  GPIO 12  ->  DRV8833 AIN1  (Coil A+)
  GPIO 13  ->  DRV8833 AIN2  (Coil A-)
  GPIO 14  ->  DRV8833 BIN1  (Coil B+)
  GPIO 15  ->  DRV8833 BIN2  (Coil B-)

--- SENSORS (per slot) ---
  GPIO  5  ->  Piezo sensor    (Slot 0)
  GPIO  7  ->  IR sensor       (Slot 0)
  GPIO 16  ->  Piezo sensor    (Slot 1)
  GPIO 17  ->  IR sensor       (Slot 1)
  GPIO 18  ->  Piezo sensor    (Slot 2)
  GPIO 19  ->  IR sensor       (Slot 2)
  Hall effect drawer sensors (3, one per slot) are wired directly to the
  ESP32, not the Pico: GPIO33/34/35 on the ESP. See ESPCode/Server/README.txt.

--- ESP UART (uart1) ---
  GPIO 21  ->  TX  (Pico -> ESP)
  GPIO 20  ->  RX  (ESP  -> Pico)

============================================================
  SLOT -> MOTOR MAPPING
============================================================
  Slot 0  ->  Motor 1  (GPIO  8-11)
  Slot 1  ->  Motor 2  (GPIO  4, 6, 27, 28)
  Slot 2  ->  Motor 3  (GPIO 12-15)
  Slot 3  ->  Motor 3  (clamped)
  Slot 4  ->  Motor 3  (clamped)

============================================================
  FREE / UNASSIGNED GPIOs
============================================================
  GPIO  0  (was DRV2 IN1, unused now Slot 1 runs on DRV4)
  GPIO  1  (was DRV2 IN2, unused now Slot 1 runs on DRV4)
  GPIO  2  (was DRV2 IN3, unused now Slot 1 runs on DRV4)
  GPIO  3  (was DRV2 IN4, unused now Slot 1 runs on DRV4)
  GPIO 22
  GPIO 26  (ADC capable)
